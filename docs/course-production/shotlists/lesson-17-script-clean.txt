# b01_intro
Alright, let's talk about compensation.

# b02_role_value
The most important thing to understand is this: compensation here is designed around the value each role is responsible for creating. And that value looks different depending on the seat you're in.

# b03_role_outcomes
For some roles, the key outcome might be qualified conversations. For others, it might be clean handoffs, signed agreements, closed revenue, team performance, operational accuracy, or something else entirely. Some plans are more fixed, some are more performance-based. It depends on the role.

# b04_no_universal_formula
That means there is no one universal formula that applies to every person forever. Your specific plan lives in your current written agreement: your offer letter, your comp plan, or your role-specific sheet. That written document is the source of truth, not this video.

# b05_scorecard_changes
As the business changes, the scorecard can change. The company may adjust which outcomes matter most, how performance is measured, and how incentives are structured. That's normal. The point is not to memorize numbers. The point is to understand the engine.

# b06_engine
The engine is simple: know what your role owns, know how success is measured right now, and do the work consistently and visibly.

# b07_current_plan
If you ever have a question about your plan, what it covers, how it works, or what applies to you, go to two places: your current written plan and your manager. Not this video, not a hallway conversation, not what someone else's plan says.

# b08_thrive
The people who thrive here aren't guessing. They know their scorecard. They know which behaviors move the business forward. They keep clean notes, follow the process, and make their work easy to see.

# b09_scoreboard
So don't think of compensation as a mystery. Think of it as a scoreboard. Your role tells you what game you're playing.

# b10_written_plan_scores
Your written plan tells you how it's scored. Your job is to play that game clearly, consistently, and professionally.
