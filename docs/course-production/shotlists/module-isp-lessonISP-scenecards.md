- Style: locked flat sticker-sheet doodle on cornflower blue `#62b3f3`; thick wobbly black outlines; flat fills only; cream/white faces; tiny dot eyes; tiny curved cast-board nose; rounded simple forms; no gradients, shadows, perspective, skin tones, or texture.
- Opening beat b01: no title card, no labels, no stickers, no word-timed text. BMH badge only.
- Reuse Lesson 2B seller cast where seller situations appear:
  - David: tired Kansas City landlord, man in his 50s, heavyset, thinning hair, short gray beard, weary.
  - Beth: inherited estate executor in St. Louis, woman in her 40s, tired but composed.
  - Ray: Dayton homeowner under payment pressure, man in his 40s, hollow-eyed but not caricatured.
  - Carol: Lake of the Ozarks lien/title problem, woman in her 60s, glasses, short curly hair, frustrated.
  - Marcus: Kansas City underwater homeowner, man in his 30s, lean, fuller dark hair, tense.
- Priya / BMH rep, if present: headset woman with black back ponytail, orange/yellow headband, orange headset with boom mic, yellow top, cream pants. Andrea is narrator only and never the seller or rep.
- **NO BLANK SHAPES (rule 1d-ii):** no card, panel, signboard, notice, or document appears anywhere in a still unless it carries its baked text as specced per beat. If a shape has no baked content and no one-sentence job, it does not exist.
- **Baked text (rule 1f):** every static prop/board/sign text below is BAKED into the generated art at generation, short CAPS, four words or fewer, drawn from that beat's VO or label queue. Judge every baked text for garbling at the image gate; garble fallback = code overlay for that text only.
- **No code-rendered scene visuals (rule 1e):** Remotion only places/composites image plates, runs slide transitions, and pops the bottom-center Sticker queue. No pure-Remotion boards, gates, forks, checkmark drawing, or diagram drawing anywhere in this lesson.
- Generated stills must contain no readable UI, no logos, no private/customer data, and no ambient doodles. The only in-art words are the baked texts specced per beat.
- The bottom-center word-timed Sticker queue is the ONLY code-rendered text. Transient teaching labels default bottom-center and use a one-at-a-time queue. The prior label clears before the next appears.
- Minimalism purpose test (rule 1d): every prop listed per beat has a stated job; generation prompts forbid additions beyond the listed props.
- Use camera-travel slide transitions only when moving to a different physical location on the blue plane. Graphic-to-graphic or checklist-to-checklist changes are straight cuts with internal pop-ins.
- Stage 3B overlap watchout: 3B cue 5-6 recaps the ISP. This lesson is the prior module that makes that recap true, so teach the full profile here and let 3B stay a quick reminder.
